# Compliance notes (to be confirmed by UAE counsel)

These notes summarise public guidance and are not legal advice.

- **Agency activity:** MoHRE distinguishes recruitment mediation from temporary employment and outsourcing. Which activity the platform performs, and the licence it needs, must be settled before any placement fee or deployment service.
- **Wages:** paid monthly as contracted, through Wage Protection System arrangements.
- **Hours:** normal private-sector hours are 8 per day or 48 per week, with overtime and rest provisions. The prototype rules engine is illustrative.
- **Health insurance:** mandatory for private-sector workers in all emirates from 1 January 2025. Occupational-injury and liability cover need separate review.
- **Personal data:** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 applies (lawful basis, purpose limitation, minimisation, security, data subject rights with exceptions). Free-zone regimes may differ.
- **Worker fees:** none. Referral incentives need legal review.
- **Funds handling:** if the platform holds client money, it needs an authorised payment partner.
