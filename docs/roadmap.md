# 90-day validation plan (targets, not results)

| Weeks | Activity | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | 15-20 interviews (contractors, providers, workers); legal view; baseline of fill time, onboarding failure, payment timing | Which trade and customer; permitted structure |
| 5-8 | Concierge pilot with one licensed provider | Two paid pilot commitments; first live requests |
| 9-12 | Build on real bottlenecks; timesheet and pay-statement trail | Time to qualified shortlist; readiness at mobilisation; wages on time |
| 13+ | Second customer and provider; test renewal and price | Contribution margin per booking; renewal intent |

Targets: two paid pilots, three or more live requests, 100-200 appropriately onboarded worker records, one renewal or paid expansion.
