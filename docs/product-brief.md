# Product brief

## Problem (founder observation, to be validated)
Industrial sites struggle to mobilise qualified, lawfully employed workers quickly. Workers report long rosters, little rest and opaque pay. These are first-hand observations. The pilot measures how widespread they are.

## Product
A booking and readiness workflow on top of licensed providers: demand and site requirements, matching from an invite-only pool, assignment-level readiness checks, timesheets the worker can review, and a wage and invoice trail kept separate.

## MVP
Build: invite-only onboarding, worker-controlled profile, credential evidence with source label, availability with last-confirmed date, readiness checklist, timesheets and pay statement, expiry alerts.

Defer: payroll processing, accommodation and transport, analytics, third-party APIs, government integrations, more trades.

Exclude: fees to workers, sale of worker data, public performance scores, blacklists, any claim of official status.

## First trades (to confirm in interviews)
Pipe fitters and mechanical technicians for industrial maintenance. Next: welders, riggers, scaffolders.

## Key open question
Can wages be paid on the contractual due date while customers pay on commercial terms, and still earn a margin?

## Kill criteria
- No positive contribution after wage-funding costs in the pilot.
- Required licensing structure is uneconomic.
- Referrals do not produce qualified, available workers.
- No paid pilot within 90 days.
- No provider joins a transparent-pay pilot.
